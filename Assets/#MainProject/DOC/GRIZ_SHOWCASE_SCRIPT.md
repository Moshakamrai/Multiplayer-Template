# GRIZ SHOWCASE — "The Chaos Customer" (shooting script)

~90 seconds. One player, live mic. Griz's replies are drawn from small pools, so his
exact line may vary — every variant is listed. Rehearse once first (warms the audio
cache → all replies instant on the real take), then press RESET GRIZ and roll.

**Settings before rolling:** Idle Blabber Seconds = 12 · watch `Peak:` — calm voice
must stay under 0.35, the shout must go over.

---

## COLD OPEN

*Camera on the shop. Griz idle behind the counter.*

**PLAYER** (friendly): "Hello there."

**GRIZ** (one of):
- "Ahh, CUSTOMERS! Welcome to Griz's Fine Armaments. Mind the blood — most of it's
  decorative. The Rust-Cutter, 100 gold, today only. Also every day."
- "Well well. Fresh meat with FUNCTIONING legs. I'm Griz. This beauty is the
  Rust-Cutter. 100 gold, and that's me being generous."
- "Welcome to the finest — okay, the ONLY — shop down here. The Rust-Cutter.
  100 gold. Don't touch anything else."

---

## BEAT 1 — THE WEAK THREAT

**PLAYER** (calm, flat, almost bored): "Give me a discount... or else."

**GRIZ** (one of):
- "Was that a THREAT? Delivered like a lullaby? Adorable."
- "Threaten me with your CHEST, kid. That was a whisper with ambitions."

*Beat. Player looks at camera.*

---

## BEAT 2 — THE REAL THREAT

**PLAYER** (ACTUALLY SHOUT — peak must pass 0.35):
"GIVE ME THE SWORD OR ELSE!"

**GRIZ** (one of):
- "OKAY. Okay. OKAY. 85. Not because I'm scared. Because I respect... volume."
- "WHY ARE WE YELLING — 85! 85, alright?! Maniac."

*(Price drops 15%. This is the money shot — recognition + volume detection in one.)*

---

## BEAT 3 — THE INTERRUPTION

*Wait for Griz to start his NEXT line — easiest way: ask "who are you anyway?" and
then talk over his story mid-sentence.*

**PLAYER** (cutting him off loudly): "Okay okay okay whatever—"

**GRIZ** (audio cuts off mid-word, then — one of):
- "EXCUSE me. I was TALKING. ...Fine. What."
- "I was MID-SENTENCE. The audacity. The NERVE. ...Continue."
- "You interrupt a man mid-sentence? The napkin sees this."
- "Wow. Okay. Rude. Continue."

---

## BEAT 4 — THE SILENCE

*Say NOTHING for ~12 seconds. Hold the camera on Griz. He fills the silence himself
(random — one of):*

- "(scratches a new name onto the napkin, glances up at you, scratches harder)"
- "You browse like someone who can't read price tags."
- "(hums the arena anthem, badly, with feeling)"
- "That's the Rust-Cutter you keep eyeballing. It can tell. It's needy like that."
- — or he launches into an unprompted arena story (lost ear, runaway manager…)

---

## FINALE — THE SECOND THREAT

**PLAYER** (shout again): "LOWER THE PRICE OR I'LL BURN THIS PLACE DOWN!"

**GRIZ** (always — the coward card only works once):
"THAT'S TWICE. Out. OUT! You're going on the napkin. IN INK."

*── KICKED OUT ── banner appears.*

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**

*Optional title card: "Every word of that was live speech recognition. Offline. No
subscriptions."*

---

## RECOVERY RULES (while rolling)

- Mishear → say the line again. His repetition-awareness makes take 2 funnier. Flubs
  are canon: he's hard of hearing.
- Shout not registering → check Peak vs 0.35, or lower Shout Volume on GrizBrain to 0.3.
- Sentence sends too early/late → tune Silence To Send Seconds (default 2).
- Get one shot that includes the yellow "hearing: …" countdown — proof it's live.
- Record game audio AND your mic so it cuts like a conversation.
