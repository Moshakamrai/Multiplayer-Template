Ravenscroft Manor. The night of the Harvest Toast.
Lord Reginald Ravenscroft — eccentric, wealthy, and just cruel enough to be interesting — announced, mid-dinner, that he was changing his will again.
He didn't say who he was cutting out. He never got the chance to.
By morning, he was found at the bottom of the east staircase. Officially, a fall.
But every single person in this house had a reason to want him gone by sunrise.
Four people were here that night. One of them is lying about more than the others.
Your job isn't to catch a lie. Everyone in this house is lying about something.
Your job is to find the one lie that killed him.
