## 🎯 CORE CONCEPT

A fusion of **rhythm-action gameplay** and **strategic card-based combat**. Players use voice commands (singing/shouting) to time their attacks and defenses to the beat of music. Every move requires precision timing AND strategic card selection, creating a unique competitive experience that rewards both rhythm skills and tactical thinking.

### Key Innovation
- **Voice-Activated Gameplay**: Shout/sing to trigger card actions on-beat
- **Real-Time Strategy**: Cards must be selected pre-round, then timed during rhythm windows
- **Asymmetric Matchups**: Rock-paper-scissors card interactions create dynamic gameplay
- **Music Integration**: Different tracks = different rhythm patterns and difficulty

## 🎮 GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

### Round Structure
1. **Shop Phase (2 minutes)**: Buy new cards and traits
2. **Round Setup**: Select 8 cards from your collection
3. **Combat Phase**: 3-5 minutes of rhythm-based battles
4. **Round End**: Award credits based on damage dealt
5. **Best of 3/5**: Multiple rounds determine match winner

### Combat System: "The Pulse"

#### Input Method: Voice Commands
- **Shout/Sing**: Trigger card action during timing window
- **Louder shout** = More damage (+25% bonus for loud voice)
- **Perfect timing (±0.1s)** = EXCELLENT (+25% damage)
- **Good timing** = GOOD (base damage)
- **Late/miss** = BAD (-50% damage)

#### Three Game Modes

**1. SLOW RHYTHM MODE**
- 1 card per beat
- 0.3-0.35 second timing windows
- Strategic, methodical gameplay
- Perfect for learning the game

**2. FAST COMBO MODE**
- Multiple cards per beat (chains of 2-5)
- Same 0.3s windows but rapid-fire
- High skill ceiling
- Intense mechanical execution

**3. CUSTOM TRACK MODE**
- Any uploaded music file
- Community-created beatmaps
- Endless replayability

## 🃏 CARD SYSTEM (31 Total Cards)

### COMBAT CARDS (20 cards) - Used in Round

#### BASIC TIER (1 Cost) - 11 Cards
Starter cards every player has access to:
- **Attacks** (4): Jab, Cross, Hook, Boom
- **Defenses** (3): Block, Dodge Left, Dodge Right
- **Special** (4): Reflect, Grapple, Fake, Clutch

**Rock-Paper-Scissors Matchups:**
- Jab beats Cross, loses to Hook
- Cross beats Hook & Block, loses to Jab
- Hook beats Jab & Block, loses to Cross
- Boom beats all attacks, loses to Block & Parry
- Block stops most attacks, loses to Grapple & Fake
- Dodge evades strikes, loses to Grapple & Uppercut

#### ADVANCED TIER (2 Cost) - 4 Cards
Strategic mid-tier options:
- **Uppercut**: Anti-dodge attack, punishes evasion
- **Sweep**: Low attack, beats Block
- **Focus**: Charge up, +50% next attack
- **Taunt**: Force opponent to attack-only next turn

#### LEGENDARY TIER (3 Cost) - 5 Cards
Powerful game-changing plays:
- **Overclock**: +35% damage, -10% self-damage (risk/reward)
- **Reverse**: Negate all damage this turn, return it
- **Trap**: Ground trap, triggers on dodge/block
- **Cage**: Opponent card trap, triggers if they play
- **Mirror**: Reflect next attack with +15% bonus

### TRAIT CARDS (10 cards) - Permanent Buffs for Entire Match

Players equip ONE trait that provides passive bonuses throughout:

#### OFFENSE TRAITS (4)
- **Piercing** (3 cost): Ignore 25% of Block defense
- **Bloodlust** (3 cost): +6% damage per consecutive hit
- **Executioner** (3 cost): +35% damage when opponent >65% health
- **Momentum** (4 cost): +10% damage per consecutive hit (max +40%)

#### DEFENSE TRAITS (4)
- **Anchored** (4 cost): Reduce stagger buildup by 40%
- **Fortress** (4 cost): -18% damage taken
- **Stalwart** (4 cost): +10% next attack after successful block
- **Regenerate** (3 cost): Heal 4% per beat

#### UTILITY TRAITS (2)
- **Quicktrigger** (3 cost): +0.1s timing window (easier execution)
- **Echo** (5 cost): 25% chance cards refresh (no cooldown next turn)

### VEX CARDS (8 cards) - Temporary Power-Ups (2 Turns)

One-time buyable buffs that boost gameplay for 2 turns:

#### OFFENSE (4)
- **Striker** (2 cost): +15% all attack damage
- **Grappler** (2 cost): Grapple +30% damage for 2 uses
- **Trickster** (3 cost): Fake/Taunt/Trap effects doubled
- **Momentum** (5 cost): +10% per consecutive hit (snowball)

#### DEFENSE (3)
- **Tank** (2 cost): -25% damage taken
- **Speedster** (2 cost): +20% timing window, -8% damage
- **Vampire** (4 cost): Heal 5% on every successful hit

#### HYBRID (1)
- **Glass** (3 cost): +40% damage, -20% defense (high risk/high reward)

### Card Economy

**Rarity Progression by Round:**

| Round | Basic | Advanced | Legendary |
|-------|-------|----------|-----------|
| 1-2   | 90%   | 10%      | 0%        |
| 3-4   | 70%   | 28%      | 2%        |
| 5-6   | 50%   | 40%      | 10%       |
| 7-8   | 30%   | 45%      | 25%       |
| 9+    | 15%   | 35%      | 50%       |

**Shop System Every Round:**
- 8 Combat card slots (rotating pool)
- 4 Trait/Vex card slots
- Each slot: BUY once per round
- Can't buy cards you already own
- Purchased slots show as "SOLD OUT"

**Credit System:**
- Earn credits based on damage dealt per round
- Higher damage = more credits to spend
- Build your deck progressively through match
